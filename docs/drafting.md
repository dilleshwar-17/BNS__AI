# Legal Drafting Engine

## Supported Documents
- FIR
- Legal Notice
- Bail Application
- Affidavit
- Consumer Complaint
- Agreement
- Civil Plaint
- Divorce Petition
- Will & Testament

## Workflow
1. Select document type
2. Fill questionnaire
3. AI generates draft with statutory references
4. Validate structure & consistency
5. Export as TXT or DOCX
